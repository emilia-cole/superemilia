Your little adventure guide

How to play

Run, double jump, and rescue a friend in each level. Five lands and 60 little adventures are ready to explore.

Move & jump

Move
← → or A D
Jump twice
Space, ↑ or W
Hold for height. Jump again in the air.
Run & use your power
Hold Shift to run.
Press E for your world power.
Explore & pause
↓ or S on a pipe opening.
P to pause or resume.

On a phone or tablet: use the arrow and Jump buttons. Tap Run to switch sprinting on or off, ↓ to enter a pipe, and the power button for a little boost.

Find the little secrets

Hit glowing ? blocks from below for stars and power-ups. Land on little grumps to bounce off them. Checkpoint flags remember your place; the big finish flag brings a friend home.

Some ordinary-looking pipes hide coin games. Stand on the opening and press ↓. Keep every coin you collect; reaching the room’s target also earns a hidden gem.

Mystery block ladder: choose one of three blocks by jumping underneath it. One holds coins! Each guess closes that row and unlocks the next tier. Double jump up, try all five tiers, then leave through the top pipe.

Boss buddies

Stage 12 of every land ends with a grumpy boss guarding a caged buddy. Bounce on its head to cheer it up: 5 bounces in Gentle mode, 7 in Adventure. Watch for shadows on the ground, because something is about to fall there, jump over the shockwaves when the boss lands, and time your moves around the land's obstacles! If you run out of hearts, the boss battle starts over from the beginning. Cheering up a boss brings its buddy home for good: Orbit from Space, Taffy from Candy, Hazel from the Forest, Bloop from Squishy World, and Biscuit from Cozy Home.

Make the adventure yours

The grand prize

Finish all 60 adventures and cheer up all 5 bosses to become a Super Emilia Superstar! You win the golden Superstar hat, a rainbow sparkle trail when you run, and 100 bonus stars.

Shop: spend stars on power-ups (a bubble shield, zoom boots, star sparkle, or a bonus 4th heart) that switch on when your next adventure starts, or on new hats. Spend coins from the secret rooms on sparkle trails, special outfit colors, more hats, or a star swap.

Open Map to choose a land and level. Use Characters to pick outfits, hair, colors, and accessories for Emilia and her buddies. Settings lets you choose which buddy comes along and switch between Gentle and Adventure mode.

You always have unlimited tries. Your coins, stars, rescued friends, and character choices save in this browser.
